    How this estimate was made

      Validate with a questionnaire (BFI-10)

      Only the owner of the profile should answer. 1 means disagree strongly and 5 means agree strongly.

        Audience authenticity

        In a natural population about 30% of follower counts start with the digit 1 and fewer than 5% start with 9 (Benford's law). Bots and bought followers tend to break that pattern.

        Samples the followers of the scraped profile, reads the follower count of each account and compares the first digits with Benford's curve. It needs at least 200 accounts and takes roughly 10 to 15 minutes. Only the numbers are saved, never the usernames.

          Digit table
          How to read this
          • Each bar is the share of accounts whose follower count starts with that digit. The line is what Benford's law predicts.
          • The chi-square p-value says how likely a gap this large is by chance in a real Benford sample. Below 0.05 is a deviation and below 0.01 is a clear one.
          • The mean deviation is the average gap between bars and line. Compare it with the value expected by chance: with a few hundred accounts even perfect data shows a small gap.
          • The law needs counts that span several orders of magnitude. When they do not, the check is unreliable.
          • Accounts with 0 followers have no first digit, so they are left out of the test, but their share is shown because it is a bot signal of its own.
          • A deviation is a reason to look closer, never proof that an account is fake.
